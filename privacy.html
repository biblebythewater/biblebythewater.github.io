Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 25, 2026

The Bible by the Water posting tool is a private tool used only by the owner of the Bible by the Water channel to upload the channel's own videos to its own accounts on YouTube and TikTok.

What we access

Only the channel's own accounts, through the sign-in each platform provides. The tool uses that access to upload the channel's videos and captions and to read the account's basic profile (name and ID) to confirm the right account is connected.

What we do not do

We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information about viewers or any other people. We do not use advertising trackers.

Storage and security

Sign-in tokens are stored privately on the channel owner's own computer and are never shared. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from the platform's account settings.

Contact

Questions can be sent through the Bible by the Water YouTube channel: youtube.com/@BiblebytheWater
